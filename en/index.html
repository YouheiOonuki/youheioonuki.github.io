Small, free tools. No sign-up. The browser tools keep what you enter in your browser.

IT admin tools

  • PAC File Tester Test a proxy.pac against a list of URLs, with syntax checks and an old/new diff. Runs offline.
  • ADSearch Get-ADUser without RSAT: a read-only PowerShell module that queries Active Directory as an ordinary domain user.
  • FILETIME & LDAP Timestamp Converter Turn 18-digit AD timestamps such as pwdLastSet and lastLogonTimestamp into dates and back.

Data converters

  • Fitbit Data to CSV Converter Turn your Google Takeout zip into one row per day: sleep, steps, resting heart rate, weight. Nothing is uploaded.
  • AI Chat Log to Markdown One Markdown file per chat from your ChatGPT or Claude export, ready for Obsidian. Nothing is uploaded.

Japanese dates and text

  • Japanese Era Converter Convert Reiwa, Heisei and Shōwa years to Western years and back. Also age, school year and a printable age chart.
  • Furigana Generator Paste Japanese text and add furigana above kanji, filtered by school grade or the Jōyō list. Runs in your browser.

Classroom and party tools

  • Bingo Caller Free, no sign-up. Big-screen draws with voice calls, plus printable cards you can check by number. Works offline.
  • Random Group Generator Split a class list into groups with rules: balance gender, keep people apart or together, avoid last time's groups. Print on A4 or Letter.
  • Unplugged Coding Printables Print a robot board, command cards and answers for screen-free coding lessons. A new puzzle every time. A4 or Letter.

For learners of Japanese

  • Hiragana & Katakana Practice Sheets Printable kana tracing sheets with romaji, and genkouyoushi grid paper. Print on A4 or US Letter, or save as PDF.

Living in Japan

  • Japan Take-Home Pay Calculator Your salary after tax in Japan: income tax, residence tax and social insurance for employees.
  • Japan Residence Tax Calculator How much residence tax you pay on your salary in Japan, and when.
  • Japan Pension Refund Calculator Lump-sum Withdrawal Payment: what you get back after leaving Japan, the 20.42% tax and your deadline.

Sky and stars

  • Web Planetarium See tonight’s sky for your city: stars, constellations, planets and the Moon’s phase. Plus an astronomy calendar and a printable planisphere.
